# AFTERPACT — Strategic Partners (analysis, not outreach)

> No company listed here has been contacted. No interest, partnership, or
> acquisition discussion exists or is implied. Categories below separate
> partners, competitors, and speculation.

## Potential commercial partners

- **PayPal / PayPal Developer ecosystem.** Natural distribution: merchants
  already reconcile PayPal transactions. AFTERPACT deepens PayPal API usage
  (Orders, Capture, Refunds, Webhooks). Risk: platform may ship native
  outcome-tracking.
- **Commerce platforms / marketplace SaaS.** Embed exception queues where
  sellers already work (listings + payouts + disputes in one surface).
- **Procurement software.** B2B milestone verification maps cleanly onto
  purchase promises.
- **Customer-service platforms.** Exception evidence + resolution records
  feed support macros and reporting.

## Potential competitors

- Pre-purchase agent-guard startups (see `COMPETITIVE_LANDSCAPE.md`) if they
  extend post-payment.
- Marketplace-native resolution flows (incumbent distribution advantage).
- Generic AI-agent ops tooling adding finance adapters.

## Hypothetical strategic-fit analysis (speculation, labeled)

A payments platform or commerce suite could value: the promise schema, the
policy engine, the eval corpus, and reference deployments. Fit would depend
on measured resolution accuracy and named traction — neither exists yet.
This paragraph is scenario planning, not a signal of any corporate interest.

## Enterprise automation vendors

Zapier-style user-authorized notifications are the only sane touchpoint today
(never bypassing policy controls). Deeper automation partnerships require
the audit story above to be proven first.
